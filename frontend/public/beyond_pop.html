The Prompt Formula — Works in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini
1
Role
Tell AI who to act as — e.g. career coach, editor, researcher
2
Context
Give relevant background about your situation
3
Task
State exactly what you want produced
4
Format
Specify how the answer should look (list, table, bullets…)
5
Review
Ask it to improve, verify, or simplify the output
💬 "Act as a career coach. I just finished my NYSC program and graduated with a degree in [field]. Give me 5 ways I can use AI to become more valuable, with one 7-day practical project."
Use the Pro-Max digital intern analogy. AI still needs context, clear instructions, examples, and supervision. Their judgment remains the final quality control.

Professional Standard

Use AI Responsibly

1
Fact-Check Everything
AI can confidently generate wrong information. Verify before submitting client work or public content.
2
Protect Privacy
Never paste confidential client, company, or personal data carelessly into public AI tools.
3
Add Local Context
Make outputs fit your audience, culture, and real conditions — not generic Western defaults.
4
Avoid Lazy Copying
Use AI as a draft partner, not your final brain. Add your voice, judgment & editing.
5
Build Evidence
Save your best prompts, projects, and results. AI output without proof of authorship is invisible.

The Salt Principle
Use the salt principle: AI is the ingredient, you are the chef. Explain that AI can confidently make mistakes, so judgment, empathy, and local context are quality control.
